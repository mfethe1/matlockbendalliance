PLTN Holdings, LLC for the PLTN Holdings Class II Landfill (IDL540000100).
The Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation (TDEC), Division of Land Protection- Waste Management Branch (DLP- WMB), has tentatively decided to issue a solid waste disposal facility permit major modification to PLTN Holdings, LLC for the PLTN Holdings Class II Landfill (IDL540000100). The facility is located in McMinn County, at
294 County Road 516, Englewood, Tennessee (latitude 35.3927/longitude -84.5171). This major modification addresses changes to the final contours, final cover system, and site drainage in order to close the landfill. This modification reduces the final contours and does not add additional air space or capacity to this landfill.
This permit would allow closure and post-closure care of the Class II disposal facility that was permitted for the disposal of excess system sand, slag, core room waste, mill room sand, core butts, pelletized plant dust, bottom drop, melt emission dust, and/or certain wastes having similar characteristics and approved in writing by TDEC. No hazardous wastes, as regulated by the Tennessee Hazardous Waste Management Act (Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 68-212-101, et. seq.) and the Rules adopted pursuant to the Act, shall be accepted for disposal at this facility. The official responsible for the operation of the facility is Monique Parker, CSP, PLTN Holdings, LLC, 42 E. Catawba Street, Belmont, NC 28012
(980) 701-9935.
DLP has made a preliminary determination that this proposal meets the applicable requirements of Tennessee Rule Chapter 0400-11-01, Solid Waste Processing and Disposal. The Class II disposal facility is in post-closure care and located at 970 Santa Fe Pike, Columbia, TN 38401 (latitude 35.649007/longitude -87.049405). The Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation’s (TDEC), Division of Land Protection – Waste Management Branch (DLP-WMB) approved a change in the identity of the owner for the Class II landfill, pursuant to Rule 0400-11-01-.02(6)(a) of Tennessee's Solid Waste Program Processing and Disposal Regulations. As part of this transfer of ownership identity, the facility name has not changed. The identity of the operator has changed from Occidental Chemical Corporation to Environmental Resource Holdings, LLC. City of Memphis Solid Waste Earth Complex Tier Two Compost Facility
The Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation (TDEC), Division of Land Protection (DLP) - Waste Management Branch (WMB), has issued a permit major modification to the City of Memphis. The Tier II compost facility, identified as Earth Complex Composting and Recycling Center (CMP790000011), is located in Shelby County at 2389 Hennington Avenue, Memphis, Tennessee (latitude 35.064473 N/longitude -90.14416 W).
This permit would allow for expansion and continuance of construction, operation, and closure of the Tier II composting facility for waste materials classified as ground yard waste from commercial landscapers, the general public, and public works organizations, as well as pre-consumer and post-consumer food waste originating from residential, commercial, and industrial sources, including, but not limited to, homes, restaurants, produce packing, animal processing, and grocery stores, and/or certain wastes having similar characteristics and approved in writing by TDEC. This major modification specifically allows compost operations to extend onto two adjacent parcels adding the potential for 9.2 acres of additional operational area. No hazardous wastes, as regulated by the Tennessee Hazardous Waste Management Act (Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 68-212-101, et. seq.) and the Rules adopted pursuant to the Act, shall be accepted for disposal at this facility. The official responsible for operation of the facility is Mr. Philip Davis, City of Memphis Solid Waste Director, 125 North Main Street, Room 628, Memphis, TN 38103 (901) 576-6872.
The WMB made the draft of this permit available for review during a 45-day public comment period which ended on June 1, 2026. The WMB received no oral or written comments during the above-mentioned public comment period.

Marion County Class I Landfill Permit Modification
The Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation (TDEC) has issued a permit major modification to the Marion County Government. The facility, identified as the Marion County Class I Landfill (SNL5800000197), is located in Marion County at 700 Chasey Simpson Road, Jasper, Tennessee (latitude 35.041667 N/longitude -85.627778 W).
This major modification corrected a construction deviation from the previous permit that resulted in the liner being constructed outside of the permitted waste limits. This major modification also resulted in an overall capacity decrease of 122,000 cubic yards to avoid conflicts with the existing transfer station at the site. This permit allows construction, operation, closure, and post-closure care of the Class I disposal facility for waste materials classified as domestic wastes, commercial wastes, institutional wastes, municipal solid wastes, bulky wastes, landscaping and land clearing wastes, industrial wastes, construction/demolition wastes, farming wastes, shredded automotive tires, dead animals, and/or certain wastes having similar characteristics and approved by TDEC. No hazardous wastes, as regulated by the Tennessee Hazardous Waste Management Act (Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 68-212-101, et. seq.) and the Rules adopted pursuant to the Act, shall be accepted for disposal at this facility.
Solid Waste made the draft of this permit available for review during a 45-day public comment period, which ended on January 26, 2026. Solid Waste received no oral or written comments during the above-mentioned public comment period.
