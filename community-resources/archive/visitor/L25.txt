Public Records Request (RDA SW35)
<p><span style="font-size: 14px;">The Tennessee Public Records Act (TPRA) grants Tennessee citizens the right to access records made or received pursuant to law or ordinance or in connection with the transaction of official business by any governmental entity that exist at the time of the request. The TPRA does not require records custodians to compile information or create or recreate records that do not exist.</span></p><p><span style="font-size: 14px;">Please check&nbsp;</span><span style="color: rgb(44, 130, 201);"><a href="https://www.tn.gov/environment/about-tdec/tdec-dataviewers.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><span style="font-size: 14px;">TDEC's online dataviewers</span></a></span><span style="font-size: 14px;">&nbsp;before submitting a request as new documents are uploaded daily. Also, many&nbsp;</span><span style="color: rgb(44, 130, 201);"><a href="https://tdec.tn.gov/filenetsearch" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><span style="font-size: 14px;">septic tank records are now available online</span></a></span><span style="font-size: 14px;">&nbsp;through a new search page.&nbsp;</span></p>
