Citizen Water Quality Complaints
Citizens may submit informal complaints about water quality violations to the department by completing our
Ask TDEC online form
or by calling our toll-free phone number 888-891-8332.
Please provide as much detailed information as possible in your request.
Citizens may also submit letters by mail to:
Division of Water Resources
Davy Crockett Tower-9th Floor
500 James Robertson Parkway
Nashville, Tennessee 37243
Please note that a written complaint submitted to TDEC is a public record subject to disclosure, including the name of the complainant.
