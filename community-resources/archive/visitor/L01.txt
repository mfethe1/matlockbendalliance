Convenience Centers
of Loudon County, Tennessee
Loudon County has three recycling and convenience centers, located in Greenback, Lenoir City, and Loudon. Please help us reduce the cost of landfill expense and save landfill space by recycling!
Operational Hours
All centers are open from
7 A.M.
to
6 P.M.
on the days shown below:
Greenback:
Tuesday through Saturday
Lenoir City:
Monday through Saturday
Loudon:
Monday through Saturday
The Convenience Centers are closed on all county holidays.

Materials
Materials Accepted
The Loudon County Recycling and Convenience Centers accept the following materials:
Cardboard
Aluminum cans
Appliances - all metals
Magazines
Newspaper
Office paper
Used motor oil and filters
Antifreeze
Computers, monitors, T.V.'s and electronics
All batteries
All plastics
Cell phones
Fluorescent light bulbs
Oil-based paint (
NOTE:
oil-based paint is ONLY accepted at the Loudon location on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday from 9AM until 2PM)
Materials NOT Accepted
The centers do not accept these materials:
Household chemicals*
Yard waste
Hazardous waste
Commercial
Demolition
Dead animals
Wood
Any biological waste
The Loudon Convenience Center at 300 Rock Quarry Road, Loudon, TN will now be accepting Household Hazardous Waste.
The Household Hazardous Waste drop off will be open on Mon, Tues, and Wed from 9AM to 2PM.
For a list of items accepted for collection, please visit
https://www.tn.gov/environment/sw-mm-household-hazardous-waste-program.html
For additional information, please call
865-988-7558
