Item acceptance / disposal call checklist

I am a household resident in [county/city]. I have [item/material], about [quantity], from [household/business origin].
Which site accepts this exact load? Please confirm the receiving address, hours (including the special HHW window), residency proof, exclusions, fees, quantity limits, appointment requirements, and unloading/accessibility help.
If you cannot accept it, which approved receiving provider should I contact? I will not travel or leave materials at the gate without confirmation.

Not sent. You must review and submit it yourself.

Private-well lab inquiry

I use a private well in [county, Tennessee] and want testing for [concern/analytes]. Do you accept household samples for this purpose?
Please confirm the panel and full price including return shipping, required bottles/preservation, collection day, numerical holding time, exact receiving address, and result turnaround.
If I need results for [agency/transaction/legal purpose], please identify the assigned lab, current Tennessee lab/method/analyte approvals and any chain-of-custody or professional sampling requirements.
I have not collected a sample or placed an order.

Not sent. You must review and submit it yourself.

Informal concern / agency routing draft

To: [verified agency and appropriate office]
Concern: [dumping / facility waste / water quality / private well]
Location: [publicly describable location or parcel/site ID]
Observed on: [date/time]
Observed facts: [what I actually saw; separate unknowns from observations]
Documentation: [safe photographs or existing report, if any]
Please confirm the appropriate complaint route, receipt/reference number and whether further information is needed. I am not asserting a cause that has not been established. If this requires a formal legal filing, please provide that route; this is an informal inquiry.
I understand written complaints and my name may become public records. [Review and remove unnecessary personal/medical information.]

Not sent. You must review and submit it yourself.

Existing public-records request draft

To: [verified county custodian or TDEC records coordinator]
I request existing [permits / inspections / complaints / monitoring records / contracts] concerning [facility/site identifier] for [specific date range].
Please provide electronic copies if available, or identify an inspection option. Please tell me before incurring any fees and provide an estimate. If another office holds these records, please identify the appropriate custodian.
I will follow the receiving agency's citizenship/identification and written-form requirements through its official route. Please do not create a new analysis for this request.
Name/contact for agency response: [add only after reviewing disclosure implications].

Not sent. You must review and submit it yourself.

CU assessment / loan inquiry draft

I own and occupy a home in [rural location/county, Tennessee] and have [private well / septic concern].
For an assessment: please confirm local service coverage, availability, any cost, included analytes, lab identity/current certification and how results will be explained. I have not collected a sample.
For a loan, if needed: please confirm current income/rural/property eligibility, available funding, interest/term, all fees, collateral and contractor-estimate requirements before I apply or begin repairs. Please provide a printed application if needed.
I will send sensitive ownership/income documents only through your confirmed provider route, not this website.

Not sent. You must review and submit it yourself.
