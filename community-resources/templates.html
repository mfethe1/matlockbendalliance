Printable working templates — unfilled and unsent

For source-backed disposal, well-testing and assistance choices, use practical visitor resources and the editable, copyable unsent drafts. These general templates do not send anything or establish a current provider service.

No forms are submitted, saved or sent by this site.

Use print (Ctrl/Cmd+P) or the browser's Save as PDF. Each field is left blank by design. Confirm the responsible provider/custodian, local requirements and prices before use. Protect household addresses, well coordinates and medical information; do not put government ID on a public page.

Environmental concern draft — do not send

To [verified authority]: privately record observed facts, dates, lawful observation location and description. No diagnosis, attribution or accusation. Private location: [keep offline and do not publish]. Photos only with consent and privacy screening.

Ask the authority to identify jurisdiction, reference number, investigation/disposition process and follow-up. Never trespass or handle unknown materials. A complaint receipt is not inspection or remedy.
