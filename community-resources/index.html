Private well testing & urgent concerns

EPA general guidance recommends routine testing and testing after a material nearby change, well repair, or changed water quality; choose a site-specific panel with a qualified adviser. A limited test cannot establish that water is “safe” or absence of landfill-related contaminants. The directory of certified laboratories is a discovery path: verify each analyte's current certification, household acceptance, container/holding rules, practical delivery, complete cost, qualified interpretation and second feasible route first. Health department free testing is not confirmed in any county.

Flooding: EPA advises staying away from a flooded well pump due to electric-shock risk and not drinking or washing with flooded-well water. Contact a verified local/state health or environmental authority and qualified well/pump professional. No DIY disinfection or treatment instructions here. General guidance, not a local emergency contact.

Records, complaints & participation

Tennessee Comptroller guidance says the governmental entity's public records policy explains how to request records, coordinator and fees. A citizen may inspect or request copies under statute subject to lawful exemptions and the custodian's policy; identification can be required. Identify records narrowly and verify the recipient and submission policy. Within seven business days, the custodian may provide records, deny in writing, or explain needed time; no guaranteed delivery date. Drafts below are intentionally UNSENT. No automatic mail, intake or request is available.
