Accessibility & privacy

Access

Guidance and core evidence are server-rendered, plain text and usable without JavaScript or external fonts. Print-friendly layouts, semantic headings, link text and visible keyboard focus are included. This local candidate has not undergone independent accessibility audit and does not claim full WCAG conformance. For an alternate format, use browser print or retain a local copy.

Privacy and disabled intake

Public submissions, alert signups and household service requests are disabled pending an accountable operator, informed consent, secure access, verified receipt, retention/deletion rules, moderation capacity and an offline alternative. This site does not collect, transmit or locally store contribution or alert information. Do not send sensitive personal, health or exact well-location information. No visitor-submission test is performed.

Limits

Research records contain source material and quotations, not proof of current conditions or local service. In an urgent situation, use an independently verified appropriate public-safety/health authority, not this unmonitored static page. Not legal, medical, contamination, safety or emergency advice.
